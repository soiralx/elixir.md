Before you start

How do I turn on Google Drive sync?

Open Settings, choose Sync, and connect your Google account. Syncing is optional.

Where are my notes stored?

In the folder you choose the first time you open elixir. Back it up like any important folder.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue on GitHub. Every message is read during the beta.
